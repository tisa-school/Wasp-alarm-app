# Wasp Buddy reference design

This experimental branch follows the supplied Wasp Buddy phone mockups while
preserving the existing camera, sound, privacy, and safety logic.

- Cream `#FFE5A3`, yellow `#FFC400`, mint `#09D7A4`, ink `#0B0B0B`, and white `#FFFFFF`
- Heavy black outlines, rounded white cards, and large circular controls match
  the simple illustrated style of the references.
- Home, camera, listening, and history screens use large labels and touch targets
  intended for young children with a grown-up nearby.
- The camera and sound tools keep honest uncertainty wording. History never says
  an area is safe or all clear because one photo cannot support that claim.
- The layout fits normal phone and desktop viewports without making the whole app
  page scroll; history results scroll inside their own list when needed.
