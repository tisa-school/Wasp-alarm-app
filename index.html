Camera & voice

The live camera needs HTTPS and browser permission. “Start camera” opens the webcam on a computer or the rear camera on a phone. “Use a saved photo instead” opens photo files.

With your permission, SCAN sends one camera photo through this app’s server to OpenAI for image analysis. Avoid including faces or personal information. This app does not save photos; the provider’s data policies still apply.

Photo checks need this app’s running server and an image-recognition service key.

Voice questions use your browser’s speech service, which may send audio to its provider. They are optional.

To keep Wasp Buddy on a phone, open this website in Safari or Chrome and choose “Add to Home Screen”.

Sound

Turn up your device volume, then tap Test sound. Phone silent modes and browser settings can affect playback.

If a child is stung, get adult help. Trouble breathing or swelling of the mouth or throat needs emergency medical help.
