# Buzz Patrol Lab design

This experimental branch tests a picture-first mission board for children around
age four. It preserves the existing camera, sound, privacy, and safety logic.

- Sky `#9DDEFF`, grass `#76D86F`, sun `#FFD43B`, coral `#FF7A59`, ink `#17312B`, paper `#FFFDF4`
- Rounded system type stays readable without downloading fonts.
- The home screen reads like an outdoor activity board: meet Buzz, choose a
  safety picture, then choose a camera or listening mission.
- Large controls, plain verbs, visible focus, and one main action per mission.
- Decorative clouds and trail marks support the outdoor theme without competing
  with safety instructions.
