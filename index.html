Buzz teaches children to stay calm, move away slowly, and find an adult.

Photo recognition can miss wasps or identify them incorrectly. It cannot measure one metre or confirm an area is safe. Stay with your child. SCAN checks one photo; it is not continuous monitoring.

Camera & voice

The live camera needs HTTPS or localhost and browser permission. “Use device camera” opens the webcam on a computer or the camera on a phone. “Choose a photo” opens your photo files.

With your permission, SCAN sends one camera photo through this app’s server to OpenAI for image analysis. Avoid including faces or personal information. This app does not save photos; the provider’s data policies still apply.

Photo checks need this app’s running server and an image-recognition service key.

Voice questions use your browser’s speech service, which may send audio to its provider. They are optional; picture questions work without a microphone.

To keep Buzz on a phone, open the hosted app in Safari or Chrome and choose “Add to Home Screen”. The downloaded HTML works as a browser app but cannot be installed from a file.

Sound

Turn up your device volume, then tap Test sound. Phone silent modes and browser settings can affect playback.
